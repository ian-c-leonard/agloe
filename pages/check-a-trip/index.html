0.0 The FILE

A standard format that represents a trip and all its nuances, readable by people and machines. It is the lasting record.

A FILE lists PERSONs, PLACEs, OPTIONs, WAYs, WANTs, FACTs and DECISIONs. The vocabulary says what each term in capitals means.

0.1 The COMPILER

            
The COMPILER checks the whole plan. If no plan works, the report names one set of records that rules every plan out.

            
The report is made for an AI agent that plans the trip, more than for a person. Its evidence is a plan, or the records that rule out every plan, or what the FILE would have to say. As data, it names each record by its id. The agent changes the FILE and checks again.

            
The first release will be tested by an agent that plans a trip alone. It writes the FILE, reads the COMPILER's reports, and ends with a FEASIBLE TRIP, with no other help.

FEASIBLE: this trip works.

Saturday 15 May 2027 (times in America/Chicago)
  2:00 pm to 3:00 pm: Museum visit, Alex and Sam
  3:00 pm to 4:00 pm: Blue Line to O'Hare, Alex and Sam
  5:00 pm to 7:30 pm: Flight home, Alex and Sam

The plan relies on these FACTs and DECISIONs:
  visit-time: Museum visit starts at 2:00 pm on Saturday 15 May 2027
  visit-length: Museum visit takes 1 h
  visit-needs-a-ticket: people need a right to take part in Museum visit
  museum-tickets: a BOOKING for Museum visit covers Alex and Sam
  train-time: Blue Line to O'Hare can start at any time
  train-length: Blue Line to O'Hare takes 1 h
  train-is-walk-up: nobody needs a right to take part in Blue Line to O'Hare
  flight-time: Flight home starts at 5:00 pm on Saturday 15 May 2027
  flight-length: Flight home takes 2 h 30 min
  bag-drop: people must be at O'Hare 45 min before Flight home starts
  flight-needs-a-ticket: people need a right to take part in Flight home
  flight-tickets: a BOOKING for Flight home covers Alex and Sam
  lock-visit: Alex and Sam take part in Museum visit
  lock-flight: Alex and Sam take part in Flight home
